# Rybelsus (semaglutide) tablets

Educational demo only. Not medical advice.

## What it's used for
Rybelsus is a prescription tablet for adults with type 2 diabetes. It is used with diet and exercise to help lower blood sugar.
It is the only GLP-1 medicine in this set that is taken by mouth as a tablet instead of an injection.
Rybelsus is not for people with type 1 diabetes and is not approved for weight loss.

## How it works
Rybelsus contains semaglutide, the same active ingredient as Ozempic and Wegovy, but in a tablet. The tablet has a special ingredient that helps semaglutide be absorbed through the stomach. Once absorbed, it acts like the hormone GLP-1: it helps the body release insulin when blood sugar is high and slows stomach emptying.

## Dosing schedule
Rybelsus is taken once a day.
- Start with 3 mg once daily for 30 days. This starting dose helps your body get used to the medicine; it is not meant to control blood sugar.
- After 30 days, increase to 7 mg once daily.
- If more control is needed after at least 30 days on 7 mg, the dose may be increased to 14 mg once daily.
Newer tablet strengths (1.5 mg, 4 mg, and 9 mg) may also be available and are not interchangeable with the older strengths [VERIFY].

## How to take it
These rules are important because food and drinks stop the tablet from working well:
- Take 1 tablet in the morning on an empty stomach, when you first wake up.
- Swallow it whole with a small sip of plain water: no more than 4 ounces (120 mL). Do not use other drinks.
- Do not split, crush, or chew the tablet.
- Wait at least 30 minutes before you eat your first food, drink anything else, or take other medicines by mouth.
- So, do not take Rybelsus with breakfast. Take it at least 30 minutes before breakfast.

## If you miss a dose
If you miss a dose, skip the missed dose. Take your next dose the next day at the usual time.
Do not take two tablets to make up for a missed dose.

## Common side effects
The most common side effects are nausea, stomach pain, diarrhea, decreased appetite, vomiting, and constipation.
Serious side effects can include pancreatitis, low blood sugar when used with insulin or a sulfonylurea, kidney problems from dehydration, serious allergic reactions, gallbladder problems, and vision changes in people with diabetic eye disease.

## Who should not take it
- People with a personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma (MTC).
- People with Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia syndrome type 2 (MEN 2).
- People who have had a serious allergic reaction to semaglutide or any ingredient in Rybelsus.
Rybelsus has a boxed warning about possible thyroid C-cell tumors.

## Interactions and special situations
- Other oral medicines: take them at least 30 minutes after Rybelsus, because they can lower how much semaglutide is absorbed.
- Levothyroxine (thyroid medicine): Rybelsus can increase the amount of levothyroxine in your body, so your doctor may check your thyroid levels.
- Insulin or sulfonylureas: higher chance of low blood sugar.
- Do not use with other semaglutide products or other GLP-1 medicines.
- Pregnancy: stop at least 2 months before a planned pregnancy [VERIFY].

## Storage
- Store at room temperature, 68°F to 77°F (20°C to 25°C).
- Keep the tablets in the original bottle (or blister pack) to protect them from moisture. Keep the bottle tightly closed.
- Do not store in the refrigerator.
- Keep out of reach of children.
