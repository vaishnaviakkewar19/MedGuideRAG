# Wegovy (semaglutide) injection

Educational demo only. Not medical advice.

## What it's used for
Wegovy is a prescription medicine used for weight management. Along with a reduced-calorie diet and more physical activity, it is used:
- to help adults and children aged 12 years and older with obesity, or adults with overweight who also have weight-related health problems, lose weight and keep it off;
- to lower the risk of heart attack, stroke, or death from heart problems in adults with known heart disease who have obesity or overweight.
It may also be used for a liver condition called MASH (metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis) in some adults [VERIFY].
Wegovy is not used to treat type 2 diabetes on its own (a different semaglutide product, Ozempic, is used for diabetes).

## How it works
Wegovy contains semaglutide, which acts like the natural hormone GLP-1. It works on areas of the brain that control appetite, so you feel full sooner and less hungry. It also slows down how fast food leaves your stomach.

## Dosing schedule
Wegovy is injected once a week. The dose is increased slowly every 4 weeks to lower stomach side effects:
- Weeks 1 to 4: 0.25 mg once weekly
- Weeks 5 to 8: 0.5 mg once weekly
- Weeks 9 to 12: 1 mg once weekly
- Weeks 13 to 16: 1.7 mg once weekly
- Week 17 and after: 2.4 mg once weekly (maintenance dose)
If an adult cannot tolerate 2.4 mg, the doctor may use 1.7 mg as the maintenance dose [VERIFY].

## How to take it
- Inject under the skin of your stomach area, thigh, or upper arm, once a week on the same day.
- You can take it at any time of day, with or without meals.
- Each pen is single-dose: it gives one dose and is then thrown away.
- You may change the day of your weekly injection as long as your last dose was at least 2 days (48 hours) before.

## If you miss a dose
- If you miss a dose and your next scheduled dose is more than 2 days (48 hours) away, take the missed dose as soon as possible.
- If your next scheduled dose is less than 2 days (48 hours) away, skip the missed dose and take your next dose on your regular day.
- If you miss doses for more than 2 weeks, talk to your doctor. You may need to restart at a lower dose to reduce stomach side effects.

## Common side effects
Common side effects include nausea, diarrhea, vomiting, constipation, stomach pain, headache, tiredness, upset stomach, dizziness, bloating, belching, gas, heartburn, and runny nose or sore throat.
Serious side effects can include pancreatitis, gallbladder problems, low blood sugar in people with type 2 diabetes, kidney problems, serious allergic reactions, increased heart rate, eye problems in people with diabetes, and depression or thoughts of suicide.

## Who should not take it
- People with a personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma (MTC).
- People with Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia syndrome type 2 (MEN 2).
- People who have had a serious allergic reaction to semaglutide or any ingredient in Wegovy.
Wegovy has a boxed warning about possible thyroid C-cell tumors.

## Interactions and special situations
- Do not use with other semaglutide products (like Ozempic or Rybelsus) or other GLP-1 medicines.
- Diabetes medicines such as insulin or sulfonylureas: there is a higher chance of low blood sugar.
- Pills taken by mouth: Wegovy slows stomach emptying, which may affect how other oral medicines are absorbed.
- Pregnancy: do not use while pregnant. Stop at least 2 months before a planned pregnancy [VERIFY].
- Surgery or procedures: tell your care team before anesthesia or deep sedation.

## Storage
- Store the pen in the refrigerator at 36°F to 46°F (2°C to 8°C).
- If needed, before removing the cap, the pen can be kept out of the refrigerator at 46°F to 86°F (8°C to 30°C) for up to 28 days.
- Do not freeze. Do not use if it has been frozen.
- Keep the pen in its original carton to protect it from light.
- Throw the pen away after use, and keep out of reach of children.
